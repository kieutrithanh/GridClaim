GridClaim creates a new digital ownership layer over the Earth using H3, GPS and OpenStreetMap.
Vision
GridClaim is the first step toward ownership in the digital world.

For thousands of years, people have owned land, homes, and assets in the physical world. As the Internet expands into augmented reality (AR), there is still no universal system that allows people to own digital locations tied to real places.

GridClaim is an experiment to explore that possibility.

Each H3 cell represents a small piece of digital space on Earth. Users can discover locations, verify their presence, and claim ownership. Over time, the value of each cell may come not only from scarcity, but also from Attention — the level of activity and interest it attracts.

Every generation has the opportunity to participate in a new kind of asset. For many people who no longer have the opportunity to enter traditional asset markets early, GridClaim is an experiment in exploring a new class of digital ownership from the beginning.

GridClaim is not a game.
It is not a cryptocurrency project.

It is an experiment in how people may own, build, and create value in the digital layer of the real world.

Beta
This Beta exists to answer one simple question:
“If the Internet becomes part of the physical world, how will people own it?”
